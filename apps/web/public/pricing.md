# SolomindLM pricing

> Start free with no credit card. Pro raises the notebook, source and daily generation limits when you need more.

Full page: https://www.solomindlm.com/pricing

## Prices

| Plan | Price | Billing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Free forever, no card required |
| Pro, billed yearly | $7.50 / month | $90 billed once a year (save 50%) |
| Pro, billed monthly | $15 / month | Billed every month |

Prices in US dollars.

## Free

Everything you need to try it on a real course.

- 5 notebooks, 20 sources each
- 10 chat messages a day
- 1 flashcard deck, quiz, report and mind map a day each
- 3 audio recaps a week
- 1 literature review every 30 days

## Pro

For a full course load, or a thesis.

- 200 notebooks, 200 sources each
- 500 chat messages a day
- 100 flashcard decks, quizzes, reports and mind maps a day
- 20 audio recaps a day
- 10 literature reviews a day
- 15 deep research runs a day
- 10 infographics a day

## Billing questions

### Is there a free plan for students?

Yes. Free accounts include notebooks with per-notebook source limits and daily caps on AI generation. Pro plans raise notebook limits. See the pricing page for current numbers.

### Is there a limit on how much I can upload?

The free plan includes 5 notebooks per account with up to 20 sources per notebook. Pro plans offer 200 notebooks per account with up to 200 sources per notebook. Each plan also includes daily limits on AI-generated content. Check our pricing page for details.
