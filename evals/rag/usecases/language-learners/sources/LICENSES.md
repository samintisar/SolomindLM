- adjectives.md: CC BY 3.0, Tex's French Grammar, pages adj2 (formation and placement), adj3 (adjectives before the noun), adj4 (irregular adjectives). Source: https://laits.utexas.edu/tex/gr/adj2.html, adj3.html, adj4.html
- passe-compose.md: CC BY 3.0, Tex's French Grammar, pages tap2 (passé composé with avoir), tap3 (with être), tap4 (pronominal verbs). Source: https://laits.utexas.edu/tex/gr/tap2.html, tap3.html, tap4.html
- pronouns.md: CC BY 3.0, Tex's French Grammar, pages pro2 (subject pronouns), pro5 (direct object pronouns), pro8 (indirect object pronouns). Source: https://laits.utexas.edu/tex/gr/pro2.html, pro5.html, pro8.html
