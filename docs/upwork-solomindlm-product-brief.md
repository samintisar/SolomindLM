# SolomindLM

## Product context for short-form video editors

**Free to start:** [solomindlm.com](https://solomindlm.com)

### What is SolomindLM?

SolomindLM is a free AI study and research notebook. Users upload lectures, PDFs, slides, notes, or research papers, then work directly from those materials instead of relying only on generic AI knowledge.

### The product story

SolomindLM helps people move from a pile of source material to a more useful learning or research workflow. A user can ask questions about uploaded materials and receive answers with citations, then turn those materials into practical study outputs.

For students, this can mean turning lectures, slides, and notes into editable flashcards, quizzes, and written practice questions. For researchers, it can mean asking questions across a reading list and creating literature-review syntheses with formatted citations, including APA, MLA, and Chicago styles.

### What the editor will be working with

We can provide product access, screen recordings, walkthroughs, source material, rough voiceovers, and creative direction. The editor's job is to shape these assets into clear, engaging vertical videos. Appearing on camera is not required. Voiceover, screen recording, text-led, hands-only, avatar, and other faceless formats are welcome.

### Short-form editing direction

- Use a format-first approach: POVs, skits, reactions, before-and-after videos, voiceovers, and screen-recording demos.
- Prioritize a strong opening hook and make the value clear quickly.
- Test multiple hooks or openings for promising concepts.
- Remove dead space and keep the pacing energetic without feeling chaotic.
- Use captions, zooms, cuts, sound design, and motion graphics when they improve understanding.
- Make the product interface easy to follow on a phone screen.
- Prefer one relatable study or research problem per video instead of a feature list.
- Create clean 9:16 exports for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. X versions may be needed occasionally.
- Help identify repeatable formats and improve them using watch time, completion, saves, shares, and clicks.

### Messages worth bringing to life

- SolomindLM works from the user's own lectures, notes, or papers.
- Answers include citations, so users can check the source.
- It can turn source material into flashcards, quizzes, and written practice questions.
- Researchers can chat across papers and create cited literature-review syntheses.
- It is free to start at [solomindlm.com](https://solomindlm.com).

### Style

The finished videos should feel useful, clear, credible, and native to short-form platforms. A relatable problem, a strong hook, and a satisfying product demonstration are more important than a heavily scripted advertisement. The goal is to make someone think, “That would save me time.”

Content can be bold, surprising, and entertaining, but it must remain accurate. We are not looking for misleading product claims or controversy for its own sake.

### Important scope note

This is an editing and post-production role. Account management, community management, paid advertising, and appearing on camera are not required.
